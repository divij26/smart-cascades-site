# Smart Dependent Dropdowns – Pricing

Plans are per monday.com account, not per user. monday.com handles billing, renewals and cancellations.

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $10 per month, or $8 per month billed yearly ($96 per year) |
| Boards | 1 | All boards |
| Cascades | 2 | Unlimited |
| Multi-level chains | Yes | Yes |
| Excel / CSV paste | Yes | Yes |
| Search in long lists | Yes | Yes |
| Users | Everyone on the account | Everyone on the account |

The Free plan has no time limit, so you can try the app on a real board before you pay.

To change plans, open the app in the monday.com marketplace and choose a plan.

Questions? Email [support@zedmere.com](mailto:support@zedmere.com).
