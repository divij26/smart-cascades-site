## Get help

Email [support@zedmere.com](mailto:support@zedmere.com). We reply within 2 business days.

Please include your board link and a short note on what you expected to happen.

## Quick start

1. Add the **Cascading Dropdown** column to your board from the column store.
2. Click a cell in that column, then click **Setup**.
3. Pick the **parent column** (a Dropdown or Status column).
4. For each parent value, tick the child options it allows. You can also paste a CSV with `parent,child` lines.
5. Click **Save**. Clicking a cell now shows only the options that fit.

## Common questions

**Who can change the setup?** Board owners and account admins. Members can pick values. Viewers and guests can't change anything.

**Is there a limit on options?** monday allows up to 1,000 labels in one dropdown column.

**What do the plans include?** Free: 1 board, 2 cascades. Pro: all boards, unlimited cascades.

**What happens when I uninstall?** We delete all of your account's setup data. Your board values and labels stay in monday.
